## Overview

**RS-MobCages** allows players to capture supported mobs inside portable Mob Cages, carry them in their inventory, transport them wherever they are needed, and release them again.

Mob Cages are designed to provide a simple and convenient way to move mobs without relying on leads, boats, minecarts, or other traditional transportation methods.
Capture a mob, take it with you, and release it at its new destination.

---

## Features

### 🐾 Capture Supported Mobs

Use an empty Mob Cage on a supported mob to capture it.
The cage stores the information needed to recreate the mob when it is released.

### 📦 Portable Mob Storage

Captured mobs can be carried as items in a player's inventory.
This makes it easy to transport mobs across your world.

### 🔄 Release Mobs Anywhere

Release a captured mob when you are ready to place it in its new location.
After release, the Mob Cage becomes empty and can be used again.

### 🧬 Mob-Specific Data

RS-MobCages uses mob descriptors to handle supported mobs and preserve mob-specific information when appropriate.
This allows different types of mobs to be handled according to their individual requirements.

### 🚫 Protected Mobs

Players and named mobs cannot be captured.
Named mobs are also protected from capture.

### 🛡️ Safe Capture Handling

If a creature is not supported by RS-MobCages, the plugin will safely prevent it from being captured.

---

## How It Works

Using a Mob Cage is simple:

1. Obtain an empty Mob Cage.
2. Hold the empty cage in your main hand.
3. Right-click a supported mob to capture it.
4. Carry the filled Mob Cage to its new location.
5. Release the mob.
6. Reuse the empty Mob Cage.
